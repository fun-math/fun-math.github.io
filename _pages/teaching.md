## Teaching at Math Olympiad Training Camps
Indian National Mathematical Olympiad (INMO) is the third stage of Mathematical Olympiad Program in India, which leads to the participation of Indian students in the International Mathematical Olympiad (IMO). Around 35 students are selected from each region after the first and second stage. An INMO Training Camp (INMOTC), is organised in each region for the selected students to train them for INMO. The top students from the INMO (approximately 65) are invited for the fourth stage, the IMO Training Camp (IMOTC). Six students are then selected to represent India at IMO.

- 2024-2025
